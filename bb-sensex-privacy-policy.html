MANDATORY FINANCIAL SIMULATION & REGULATORY DISCLAIMER

BB Sensex Paper Trading is exclusively a virtual paper-trading simulation and educational tool designed to help students, retail learners, and trading enthusiasts understand the mechanics of BSE SENSEX 30 index movements and options trading without risking real capital.

  • Zero Real Money Trading: The app does NOT place real orders on the Bombay Stock Exchange (BSE) or National Stock Exchange (NSE). It does not interface with actual trading accounts or depositories (NSDL/CDSL).
  • Zero Real Financial Value: All cash balances, trading capital, profits, losses, Ad Coins, mystery box awards, and portfolios inside this app are 100% virtual and have zero monetary or real-world cash value. They cannot be withdrawn, transferred, refunded, or redeemed for real money.
  • Not a SEBI Registered Entity: Aviraaj Digitech and this application are NOT registered with SEBI (Securities and Exchange Board of India) as an Investment Advisor, Portfolio Manager, Broker, or Research Analyst. No content, chart, option chain calculation, or leaderboard inside this app constitutes financial, legal, tax, or investment advice.

1. Introduction & Overview

At Aviraaj Digitech ("we," "our," or "us"), we are committed to upholding the highest standards of data safety, operational integrity, and user privacy. This Privacy Policy details our procedures regarding the collection, use, retention, and safeguarding of information when you download, install, and use BB Sensex Paper Trading (the "App") from the Google Play Store.

By accessing or using the App, you consent to the data practices described in this Privacy Policy. If you do not agree with this policy, please do not access or use our services.

2. Information We Collect

We adhere to the principle of Data Minimization. We collect and store only the data strictly essential to operate a synchronized virtual trading simulator:

  • Account Authentication Credentials (Google Sign-In via Firebase Auth): When you log into the App using Google One-Tap Sign-In, we receive your public Google Profile information:
    • Display Name (used for your in-app trader badge and public leaderboard rank).
    • Email Address (used strictly as your unique account identifier and for account recovery).
    • Google Profile Avatar URL (optional visual avatar displayed inside your app profile).
    • Unique Firebase User ID (UID) (used to securely index your virtual portfolio and trading ledger).
  • Simulated Financial & Trading Ledger Data: To allow seamless persistence across sessions, we save your paper trading activity in Google Firebase Realtime Database:
    • Virtual portfolio balance, margin utilized, and realized profit/loss.
    • Open and historical paper positions (Option type, strike, quantity, entry price, exit price, timestamps).
    • Automated Stop-Loss and Target Price trigger settings.
    • Virtual Ad Coin tally, trader tier levels, and mystery box progression.
  • Community Interaction Data: Messages voluntarily sent by you in the global community chat room, along with your display name and timestamp.
  • Technical Device Identifiers & Analytics: Non-sensitive diagnostic telemetry including device model, Android OS version, app build version, and anonymized performance crash logs.

3. Third-Party SDK Integrations & Disclosures

The App utilizes vetted third-party software development kits (SDKs) to power core cloud and monetization capabilities. These providers operate under their respective privacy protocols:

  • Google Firebase (Google LLC): Powers user authentication, database persistence, and crash telemetry. All data transmissions between the App and Firebase servers are encrypted in-transit using 256-bit TLS/SSL encryption.
    Google Firebase Privacy & Security Standards
  • Google AdMob (Google LLC): We display banner ads and user-initiated Rewarded Video Ads (which users can voluntarily watch to earn virtual Ad Coins or trading unlock passes). Google AdMob utilizes the device's Google Advertising ID (AAID/GAID) to prevent fraudulent clicks, limit ad repetition (frequency capping), and serve non-personalized or personalized advertisements based on user consent.
    Google Advertising Policies
  • Upstox Market Feed API: The App reads publicly available live market closing quotes and option chain structures (strike prices, LTP, Open Interest, volume) for educational reference. No user information, personal email, or trading activity is ever shared with or transmitted to Upstox.

4. How We Use Collected Information

We process collected information strictly for operational and educational purposes:

  • To manage your virtual paper portfolio and sync trades across app relaunches.
  • To calculate realistic options profit and loss based on live BSE Sensex price movements.
  • To display anonymous competitive ranks on the global trader leaderboard.
  • To reward virtual coins and unlock paper trading sessions upon completion of rewarded video ads.
  • To moderate community chat messages and filter spam, vulgarity, or prohibited content.
  • To prevent fraud, multiple account abuse, and bot activity.

5. App Permissions Disclosed

The App requests only the standard Android permissions necessary for basic operation:

  • android.permission.INTERNET: Required to stream market quotes from Upstox, sync Firebase portfolios, and load AdMob advertisements.
  • android.permission.ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE: Required by Firebase and AdMob to detect whether an active data connection is available.
  • android.permission.VIBRATE: Used for haptic feedback during trade executions and button presses (can be disabled in phone settings).
  • No Dangerous Permissions: We do NOT ask for or access Camera, Microphone, GPS Location, Contacts, SMS, Phone Logs, or External Device Storage.

Your information is only processed through encrypted infrastructure (Google Firebase, Google AdMob) strictly to deliver the App's functionalities.
