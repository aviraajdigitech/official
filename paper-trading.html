What is Paper Trading and Why Every Indian Trader Needs It?

In the fast-moving world of the Indian financial markets (NSE & BSE), jumping straight into live trading with hard-earned capital is the primary reason why over 90% of beginner intraday and F&O traders incur heavy losses.

Paper Trading (Virtual Trading) is the ultimate training ground. It provides a real-time simulated environment where you execute buy and sell orders on live market prices—such as Nifty 50, Bank Nifty, FinNifty, and Top Equity Stocks—using virtual funds instead of real money.
