1. Commitment to User Privacy

At Aviraaj Digitech ("we," "our," or "us"), we prioritize the privacy and fundamental data protection rights of all users, visitors, and clients interacting with our applications, websites, and algorithmic software tools. This Privacy Policy details how information is collected, processed, and safeguarded.

2. Information We Collect

We collect and process minimal data necessary for operational stability and service delivery:

  • Direct User Inquiries: Name, work email address, phone/WhatsApp number, and project requirements submitted voluntarily via our contact forms or customer support channels.
  • Application Telemetry: Anonymized device metrics, operating system version, crash diagnostics, and screen resolution to maintain 60fps mobile performance and bug-free execution.
  • Financial Simulation & Game Profiles: In educational trading simulators (such as BullBearMarket.in), user handles, virtual coin balances, and prediction leaderboard achievements are recorded purely for gameplay and educational simulation.
  • Wealth Calculator Data: In utility applications like Wealth Planner Pro, all financial calculations (SIP, Step-Up SIP, Inflation) are processed entirely on-device without storing or uploading sensitive financial numbers to external servers.

3. Purpose and Legal Grounds of Data Processing

We process collected information solely for legitimate operational purposes:

  • To operate, test, and enhance our mobile applications and high-speed web portals.
  • To provide prompt technical support and client communication via email and WhatsApp.
  • To prevent fraud, automated abuse, and ensure cybersecurity across our server infrastructure.
  • To comply with legal obligations under the Digital Personal Data Protection (DPDP) Act 2023 and Information Technology Act 2000 of India.

4. No Sale or Commercial Exploitation of Personal Data

Aviraaj Digitech does not sell, rent, monetize, or trade your personal data to third parties or advertising brokers. Data is used strictly within the bounds of delivering our software and services.

5. Compliance with Google Play Developer Policy

All Android applications distributed under the Aviraaj Digitech developer console adhere strictly to Google Play Developer Program Policies:

  • Data Safety: Accurate disclosures regarding data collection, transmission, and encryption standards.
  • Children's Privacy (COPPA): We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under the age of 13. Educational trading simulators are designed for mature learning.
  • Account & Data Deletion: Users have the permanent right to request full deletion of their account data, profiles, and associated records by contacting our support team.

6. Data Security and Technical Safeguards

We implement robust administrative, technical, and physical security measures, including 256-bit SSL transport layer encryption, secure hosting environments, and access-controlled databases to prevent unauthorized access or data exposure.

7. Updates to this Policy

We may revise this Privacy Policy periodically to reflect new features, app updates, or regulatory enhancements. Updates will be posted on this page with an updated effective date.
