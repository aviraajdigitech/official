1. Commitment to User Privacy

At Aviraaj Digitech ("we," "our," or "us"), we prioritize the fundamental privacy rights and digital safety of all users, players, and visitors interacting with our mobile applications and digital platforms. We build software centered on user trust, minimal data footprint, and total operational transparency.

This Privacy Policy details how data is handled across our applications, with explicit operational disclosures for our flagship games—Logic Maze and Missiles Blast: Fighter Jets—as well as our financial simulation tools and utility applications.

2. Disclosures for "Logic Maze" Puzzle Game

For players enjoying Logic Maze (including Arrow Snake Puzzle, Gem Slide, and Neon Flow game modes / Package: com.bullbearmarket.arrowpuzzlego), we operate on a strict Offline-First & Zero Personal Data architecture:

  • No Account Required: Players can install and play the complete game without creating an account, registering an email, logging in with social profiles, or providing their phone number.
  • Local Progression Storage: All gameplay progress (stars earned, levels completed, coins, gems, inventory boosters like Bomb, Turn, Hint, and Undo) is stored 100% locally on your device using client-side application storage (localStorage). We do not transmit or store your game save states on remote external databases.
  • Zero Sensitive Data Collection: Logic Maze does not read, access, or harvest your personal contacts, photos, media files, device microphone, camera, or geographic GPS coordinates.

3. Disclosures for "Missiles Blast: Fighter Jets" Arcade Game

For pilots playing Missiles Blast: Fighter Jets (Package: com.missilesblast.game), our architecture is equally built on a strict Offline-First & Zero Personal Data privacy model:

  • No Account or Sign-Up Mandatory: You can download, launch, and play all dogfight survival modes, fly supersonic jets, and dodge missiles completely offline without ever signing up, entering an email, or sharing personal credentials.
  • Local Game Progress & Asset Storage: High scores, flight statistics, collected stars, unlocked fighter aircraft (such as Viper Strike, Falcon 35, Raptor X, Night Prowler, and Berkut 47), shields, speed boosts, audio preferences, and control settings are stored 100% locally on your device via client-side storage (localStorage). No gameplay telemetry or personal save files are transmitted to our private servers.
  • Google Play Games Integration (Optional): Players may optionally connect their Google Play Games profile to sync global high score leaderboards and achievements. This authentication is handled directly and securely through Google Play Services APIs. Aviraaj Digitech does not collect, harvest, or store your Google credentials.
  • Zero Sensitive Permissions: Missiles Blast does not request or access sensitive device components such as Camera, Microphone, GPS location, Contacts, SMS, or External Device Storage.

4. Third-Party Advertising & Google AdMob Disclosures

To offer Logic Maze and Missiles Blast: Fighter Jets as free games to players worldwide and support ongoing updates, our games integrate the Google AdMob SDK (provided by Google LLC).

Google AdMob may collect and process certain non-sensitive diagnostic and advertising telemetry:

  • Device & Advertising Identifiers: The Google Advertising ID (AAID on Android) is utilized by AdMob to deliver contextual ads, limit ad frequency (frequency capping), and measure ad effectiveness.
  • Diagnostics & Performance Data: Anonymized crash logs, app responsiveness metrics, and network connection types to prevent invalid traffic and detect ad fraud.
  • Ad Formats Used: We utilize banner advertisements, occasional interstitial transitions between flight missions/puzzle milestones, and user-initiated Rewarded Video Ads (which players voluntarily choose to watch to earn in-game revives, shields, or booster power-ups).

Ad Personalization Control & Opt-Out: Players have complete control over advertising personalization. You can easily reset your Advertising ID or opt-out of personalized interest-based advertising directly from your Android device settings by navigating to:
Settings > Google > Ads > Reset advertising ID or Delete advertising ID.

To learn more about how Google processes advertising data, please review Google’s official documentation:
Google Advertising Policies & Technologies | Google Privacy Policy

5. Information We Collect Across Our Portfolio

Across our broader digital software portfolio, we collect and process only the minimal data necessary for operational stability:

  • Customer Support & Direct Inquiries: Your name, work email address, and WhatsApp message communications submitted voluntarily when requesting technical assistance or business inquiries.
  • Device Telemetry: Anonymized OS version, screen resolution, and crash logs to maintain smooth 60fps performance and bug-free execution across Android hardware variants.
  • Financial Simulation Profiles: In educational simulators (BullBearMarket.in, BearBull Crypto Simulator), virtual paper coins and leaderboard nicknames are stored purely for learning with zero real-money financial involvement.
  • On-Device Calculators: In utilities like Wealth Planner Pro, all financial estimations (SIP, CAGR, inflation calculations) execute entirely on-device without sending financial numbers to servers.

6. App Permissions Disclosed

When installing Missiles Blast: Fighter Jets, Logic Maze, or our Android applications, only strictly necessary system permissions are requested:

  • android.permission.INTERNET: Required solely to load advertising assets via the Google AdMob SDK and optional Google Play Games leaderboard synchronization.
  • android.permission.ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE: Required to verify whether an active internet connection is available before attempting to load an ad or cloud leaderboard.
  • android.permission.VIBRATE: Utilized to deliver subtle haptic tactile feedback during missile explosions, near-miss evasions, or interactive UI clicks (can be toggled ON/OFF inside game settings).
  • No Dangerous Permissions: We do NOT request permissions for Camera, Microphone, GPS Location, Contacts, SMS, or External Storage.

7. Purpose and Legal Grounds of Data Processing

We process collected telemetry and support communications strictly for legitimate operational purposes:

  • To operate, test, update, and maintain our mobile games and digital tools.
  • To serve contextual in-game ads via Google AdMob and prevent advertising fraud.
  • To provide prompt technical support and resolve player feedback via email and WhatsApp.
  • To safeguard cybersecurity, prevent automated spam, and ensure server infrastructure integrity.
  • To comply with regulatory standards under the Digital Personal Data Protection (DPDP) Act 2023 and Information Technology Act 2000 of India.

8. No Sale or Commercial Exploitation of Personal Data

Aviraaj Digitech does NOT sell, rent, monetize, trade, or distribute your personal information to third-party data brokers. Any data processed is utilized strictly within the direct delivery of our applications and transparent advertising frameworks.

9. Compliance with Google Play Developer Program Policies

All Android applications published under the Aviraaj Digitech Google Play Console strictly conform to the Google Play Developer Program Policies:

  • Google Play Data Safety: Complete and accurate disclosure of all SDK dependencies (Google AdMob, Google Play Games), data transmission encryption (HTTPS/TLS), and offline device data storage.
  • Children’s Privacy (COPPA) & Families Policy: Both Logic Maze and Missiles Blast: Fighter Jets are designed for general audiences as family-friendly games. We do not knowingly solicit or collect personally identifiable information from children under the age of 13. If you believe any minor has provided personal data to us, please notify us immediately for prompt deletion.
  • Transparent Ad Placement: Ads displayed adhere strictly to Google Play’s Better Ads Standards. Ads do not interfere with normal gameplay, do not trigger unexpectedly during intense action/puzzle sessions, and rewarded ads are 100% opt-in.

10. Data Retention & User Deletion Rights

We respect your complete autonomy over your digital footprint:

  • In-App Data Reset: Players can clear all local high scores, unlocked jets/booster saves, and settings at any time directly through device app settings (Clear Storage / Clear Cache). In Logic Maze, players can also use Settings > Options > Wipe Progress.
  • Uninstallation: Uninstalling the application automatically removes all locally stored game files, scores, and cached data from your device.
  • Support Inquiries: Any support communications or emails sent to us can be permanently deleted upon your request by emailing our Data Protection Team at official@aviraajdigitech.com.

11. Data Security & Technical Safeguards

All communications between our mobile apps, AdMob SDKs, Google Play Services, and our official web servers utilize enterprise-grade 256-bit SSL/TLS transport layer encryption (HTTPS). We implement rigorous defensive safeguards to protect against unauthorized data tampering, interception, or unauthorized exposure.

Any modifications will be posted directly to this URL (https://aviraajdigitech.com/privacy.html) with an updated revision date. We encourage players to review this page periodically.
