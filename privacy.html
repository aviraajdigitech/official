1. Introduction

Aviraaj Digitech ("we," "our," or "us") is dedicated to safeguarding the privacy and personal data of our users, clients, and platform visitors. This Privacy Policy outlines the types of information we collect, how it is processed and protected, and your rights regarding your personal information across all our digital properties.

2. Information We Collect

We may collect information in the following circumstances:

  • Directly Provided Information: Contact details such as your name, email address, phone number, and project specifications submitted via our contact forms or service inquiries.
  • Technical & Analytical Data: IP address, device type, browser specifications, operating system, and anonymous usage telemetry collected to maintain site security and optimize user experience.
  • Application Data: In our financial learning and market simulation apps (such as BullBearMarket.in), user profile identifiers and simulation balances are processed securely to render leaderboard scores and portfolio simulations.

3. Purpose of Data Processing

Your data is processed strictly for legitimate operational purposes, including:

  • Delivering, maintaining, and enhancing our web applications and mobile software.
  • Responding to technical inquiries, customer support requests, and business proposals.
  • Ensuring cybersecurity, fraud prevention, and compliance with applicable legal standards.
  • Optimizing system performance and server reliability.

4. Data Protection & Security Standards

We implement industry-grade technical and organizational security measures, including 256-bit SSL encryption in transit, secure cloud hosting with DDoS mitigation, and restricted administrative access protocols. We do not sell, rent, or trade personal data to third parties.

5. Third-Party Services & Links

Our web properties and applications may contain links to external websites, APIs (such as market data providers), or cloud services. We encourage users to review the privacy policies of any third-party platforms they interact with.

6. Compliance with App Store & Google Play Guidelines

All mobile applications developed and distributed under the Aviraaj Digitech developer profile adhere strictly to Google Play Developer Policy and Apple App Store Review Guidelines regarding user privacy, data minimization, and transparency.
